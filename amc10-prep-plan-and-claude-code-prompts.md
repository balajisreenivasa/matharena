# AMC 10 A/B 2026 Prep: Plan, Materials, Daily Schedule, and Claude Code Prompts

**Test dates:** AMC 10A Thu Nov 5, 2026 · AMC 10B Fri Nov 13, 2026
**Runway from Mon Sep 21:** 45 days to 10A, 53 days to 10B (about 6.5 weeks)
**Format:** 25 multiple choice, 75 min, no calculator. 6 pts correct, 1.5 blank, 0 wrong.

> Action item this week: she needs a seat. Families can't register directly; find a school or test center hosting BOTH dates. Centers fill up and many close sign-ups in mid-October.

## 1. Goal and scoring strategy

| Target | Score | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Achievement Roll (grade 8 and below) | 90+ | 12 correct + 13 blank = 91.5 |
| Typical AIME cutoff zone | ~95 to 105 | 14 correct + 11 blank = 100.5 |
| Stretch | 110+ | 16 correct + 9 blank = 109.5 |

The whole plan is built around one idea: **be near-perfect on problems 1 to 15, pick up 2 to 4 from 16 to 20, and leave the rest blank unless she can eliminate down to two choices.** A wrong answer costs 1.5 points versus a blank, so guessing blind from five choices is break-even at best; guessing from two or three is worth it.

The diagnostic on Day 1 sets the real target. If she scores under 60, the plan shifts to weight problems 1 to 12; over 90, it shifts to 16 to 22.

## 3. Daily structure

**Weekdays, 70 min**
| Block | Min | What |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | 10 | Review queue: problems she missed 1, 3, 7, 14 days ago |
| Lesson | 20 | One concept, 2 to 3 worked examples |
| Worksheet | 30 | 8 problems, ordered easy to hard, timed |
| Corrections | 10 | Enter answers, read solutions for misses, tag the error type |

**Saturday, 90 min:** timed mock or topic quiz, real conditions (paper, pencil, no calculator, 75 min, bubble sheet).
**Sunday, 30 to 45 min:** mock review only. Redo every miss without looking at the solution first.

Cap it there. An 8th grader doing more than this for seven weeks straight burns out before Nov 5.

---

## 4. Error tagging (this drives the adaptation)

Every missed or skipped problem gets one tag:
- **C: Concept** didn't know the idea → lesson gets re-queued, easier problems on that subtopic
- **S: Setup** knew the idea, couldn't start or model it → more problems of same type, same difficulty
- **E: Execution** arithmetic/algebra slip → accuracy drills, "check your answer" habit
- **R: Misread** answered a different question → underline-the-question habit
- **T: Time** ran out → speed sets on problems 1 to 10

Plus, for every problem, a confidence mark (sure / unsure / guessed). "Sure and wrong" is the most valuable signal there is.

## 6. How the site should adapt (the logic, in plain terms)

1. **Every problem has:** year, test, number, topic, subtopic, difficulty band (D1 = #1 to 5, D2 = #6 to 10, D3 = #11 to 15, D4 = #16 to 20, D5 = #21 to 25), answer, link to the solution page.
2. **Every subtopic has a mastery score (0 to 1) per student**, updated after each answer. Correct on a hard problem moves it up more than correct on an easy one; "sure and wrong" moves it down more than "unsure and wrong."
3. **Each daily worksheet = 8 problems:**
   - 4 from today's lesson subtopic, at her current band for that subtopic (start one band below, step up after 3 of 4 correct, step down after 1 of 4)
   - 2 from her weakest subtopics already taught
   - 1 from the review queue (previously missed, due today by spaced repetition: 1, 3, 7, 14 days)
   - 1 confidence builder from a strong subtopic, to keep speed up
4. **Error tags change what comes next:** C re-queues the lesson and drops a band; S keeps the band and adds same-type problems; E/R adds a 5-problem accuracy sprint to the warm-up; T adds a timed D1/D2 speed set.
5. **Mock results re-plan the following week:** any subtopic under 50% in a mock gets one of the two "weakest" slots for the next five worksheets.
6. **Never serve a problem from a reserved mock test** until that mock is done.
7. **Parent view:** projected score (from band-level accuracy), mastery heatmap, streak, time per problem, error-tag breakdown.

### Prompt 2: Student model and adaptive engine

```
Build the adaptive engine as a pure, well-tested module (no UI in this step).

DATA: Student, Attempt (student, problem, chosen_answer or blank, correct, seconds_spent, confidence: sure|unsure|guessed, error_tag: C|S|E|R|T|null, source: worksheet|mock|review, timestamp), SubtopicMastery (student, subtopic, score 0-1, current_band, attempts, last_seen), ReviewItem (student, problem, due_date, interval_index).

MASTERY UPDATE after each attempt: Elo-style. Treat each band as a fixed rating (D1 lowest, D5 highest); student has a rating per subtopic; update with K larger for the first 10 attempts in a subtopic. Modifiers: sure+wrong = 1.5x penalty; guessed+correct = 0.5x gain; blank = small penalty. Expose score as 0-1 via logistic transform.

BAND STEPPING per subtopic: start one band below the student's diagnostic level. Step up after 3 of last 4 correct at the current band; step down after 1 of last 4.

REVIEW QUEUE: every incorrect or blank attempt creates a ReviewItem due in 1 day; on correct redo advance to 3, 7, 14 days then retire; on incorrect redo reset to 1.

ERROR-TAG EFFECTS:
- C: re-queue that subtopic's lesson within 2 days, drop one band
- S: keep band, add 2 same-subtopic problems over the next 3 worksheets
- E or R: add a 5-problem D1/D2 accuracy sprint to the next warm-up
- T: add a timed D1/D2 speed set (10 problems, 15 min) within 3 days

WORKSHEET GENERATOR generate(student, date) returns 8 ordered problems:
- 4 from the day's scheduled lesson subtopic(s) at current band
- 2 from the two lowest-mastery subtopics already taught
- 1 due ReviewItem (if none due, another weak-subtopic problem)
- 1 from a high-mastery subtopic at current band (confidence/speed)
Rules: never use reserved_for_mock problems unless that mock is completed; never repeat a problem outside the review queue; order easy to hard; on "adaptive" days with no lesson, use 5 weak + 2 review + 1 strong.

MOCK RE-PLAN: after a mock, any subtopic under 50% accuracy is pinned into the weak slots for the next 5 worksheets.

PROJECTED SCORE: from band-level accuracy, estimate expected correct count and the optimal blank strategy (6 correct / 1.5 blank / 0 wrong); output score and a recommended "attempt through problem N" number.

Deterministic given a seed. Heavy unit tests, including a simulated 45-day student to confirm bands move sensibly and no reserved problem leaks. Stop when tests pass and show me the simulation summary.
```
